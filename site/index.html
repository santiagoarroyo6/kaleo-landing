FAQ

When is the Grand Opening?

Friday 20 November, from 10 PM to 3:30 AM.

Is KALEO still at Barrio Cellar?

No. KALEO’s Barrio Cellar era ended with The Last Dance on 6 November. From Friday 20 November, KALEO’s new home is downstairs at Cheers Bar, 561 George St.

How do I get there?

561 George St, Sydney — downstairs at Cheers Bar. It’s a short walk from Town Hall Station.

Is it 18+?

Yes. It’s a licensed venue: everyone needs to be 18 or over with valid photo ID.

What music is played?

Reggaeton and Latin all night, mixed with international hits.
